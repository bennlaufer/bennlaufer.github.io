07 / quality bar

Risks, guardrails, and acceptance criteria

Professional positioning only works if the page is accurate, shareable, and connected to the rest of the site.

Risks to manage

  • Attribution risk: do not imply employment, ownership, or endorsement beyond the confirmed collaboration.
  • Content risk: the repository has only a short description today; new findings must come from the paper or user-provided source material.
  • Data risk: avoid participant-level details, restricted methods, or unpublished results.
  • Duplication risk: keeping the old resume project and the new experience entry would make the portfolio feel inconsistent.
  • Navigation risk: changing category without adding internalUrl would leave the current broken GitHub fallback in place.
Guardrail: use the phrase “Westat collaboration” throughout until the exact relationship and role title are confirmed.

Definition of done

  • Westat appears under /projects/professional and no longer under /projects/academia.
  • /projects/westat renders with a descriptive title, SEO metadata, responsive layout, and working paper link.
  • The homepage Experience section includes one concise Westat entry with a working case-study link.
  • The homepage timeline places Westat after Cal Poly research and before Postal.io with accurate collaboration wording.
  • No Westat card points to https://github.com/bennlaufer/ or another empty fallback.
  • npm run build passes and affected routes work at desktop and mobile widths.
  • Content review confirms every claim is public, accurate, and approved for portfolio use.
